## How to Use Templates

### Selecting a Template

1. **Click "Template" button** on any resume card in My Resumes
2. **Browse template options** with previews
3. **Click "Preview"** to see the full template
4. **Click "Select"** to choose your template
5. **Export as PDF** using your chosen template

## ATS Compatibility

### What is ATS?
Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are software used by large companies to automatically parse and filter resumes. They look for:
- Standard formatting
- Relevant keywords
- Clear section headers
- Parseable text

### ATS-Optimized Template Features
✅ Simple, clean formatting  
✅ Standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman)  
✅ No tables or graphics  
✅ Standard section headers  
✅ Consistent spacing  
✅ No special characters  
✅ Easy to parse text  

### Tips for ATS Success
1. **Use standard section names:** EXPERIENCE, SKILLS, EDUCATION
2. **Include keywords:** Match job description keywords
3. **Use simple formatting:** Avoid tables, graphics, columns
4. **Standard fonts:** Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman
5. **Consistent spacing:** Regular line breaks and indentation
6. **No special characters:** Avoid symbols, graphics, lines

## Export Tips

### Before Exporting
1. ✅ Choose appropriate template for the role
2. ✅ Review your resume content for accuracy
3. ✅ Check formatting and spacing
4. ✅ Verify all contact information
5. ✅ Proofread for typos and grammar

### Export Settings
- **Format:** PDF (recommended for all templates)
- **File Name:** Use professional naming: `FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf`
- **Font Embedding:** Ensure fonts are embedded in PDF

### After Exporting
1. ✅ Open PDF to verify formatting
2. ✅ Check that all text is readable
3. ✅ Ensure no text is cut off
4. ✅ Test on different devices
5. ✅ Share with confidence

## Troubleshooting

### Template Not Showing Correctly
- Refresh your browser
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different template
- Check your resume content for special characters

### PDF Export Issues
- Ensure your resume content is complete
- Check for very long lines that might wrap
- Try a different template
- Use a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)

### Content Not Fitting
- Condense your descriptions
- Reduce font size (if supported)
- Try Minimal Clean template
- Remove less relevant information

### ATS Parsing Issues
- Use ATS-Optimized template
- Avoid special characters and symbols
- Use standard section names
- Keep formatting simple

## FAQ

**Q: Which template should I use?**  
A: Use ATS-Optimized for online applications, Modern Minimalist for tech companies, Classic Professional for corporate roles, and Creative Modern for creative positions.

**Q: Can I customize templates?**  
A: Yes! Edit your resume content in the resume editor, and the template will format it accordingly.

**Q: Are templates mobile-friendly?**  
A: Templates are optimized for PDF export. Mobile viewing may vary, but PDFs display consistently.

**Q: Do all templates work with ATS?**  
A: ATS-Optimized is specifically designed for ATS systems. Others are ATS-compatible but not optimized.

**Q: Can I switch templates after exporting?**  
A: Yes! Select a different template and export again. Each export uses your current template selection.

**Q: Which template is most professional?**  
A: Classic Professional and Minimal Clean are most formal. Choose based on industry.

**Q: Can I use the same template for all applications?**  
A: Yes, but tailoring templates to the role increases your chances of success.
